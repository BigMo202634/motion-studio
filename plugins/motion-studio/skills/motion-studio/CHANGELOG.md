# Changelog

Every update gets a version number and a short list here. The newest is at the top.
Version numbers: 1.0.1 = a fix, 1.1.0 = something new, 2.0.0 = something that works differently.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-27)
First public release.
- Motion runtime (`motion.js`): smooth, frame-exact animations rendered to MP4 with sound.
- 176 real sound effects (whooshes, pops, counters, cash register, alerts, crowd...), downloaded once with `scripts/get_sounds.py`.
- Number counters with ticking sounds that follow the count.
- 41 trending fonts (Geist, Inter, Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif, Anton, Nunito...).
- Templates: 15s vertical promo, 20s landscape launch film, real estate talking-head reel.
- Real estate look: one big lime keyword at a time sitting behind the agent, small white words, agent photo card, price counter, photo cards, map, "calling" phone CTA.
- Captions from the agent's own speech (`scripts/transcribe.py`) and person cut-out (`scripts/matte.py`).
