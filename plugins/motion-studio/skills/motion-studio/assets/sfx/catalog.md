# Sound library (176 real recorded effects)

Sources: Mixkit (Mixkit Sound Effects Free License) and Pixabay (Pixabay Content License).
Both are free for commercial and personal videos with no credit required. Neither allows redistributing the raw files on their own, so keep this folder private.
Leading and trailing silence is trimmed, so every sound starts on its cue frame. Long beds (applause, ticking, typing) are capped at 6–8s with a fade.
Use the name without extension in `MS.cue(at, name, gain)`.
